A first ski lesson is mostly a lesson in falling over and getting back up, and only then in skiing. That is normal, and it passes quickly.

## The first hour

Your instructor will start without poles: walking on one ski, feeling the edge, and sliding a short distance on a gentle slope.

## Why mornings win

Morning snow is firmer and faster, and that actually helps — a ski glides far more easily on firm snow than on afternoon slush.
